# Brag Plan: Media Octus CRM

## What is this app?
A full-stack, enterprise-grade Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising CRM that unifies billboard inventory, lead-to-quotation pipelines, campaign scheduling, and proof of display in one platform.

## The angle
Traditional OOH media companies run on chaotic WhatsApp groups, messy Excel sheets, and delayed photo verifications. Media Octus replaces the mess with automated vacancy tracking, instant PDF quotes, and verified proof-of-display workflows.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A stark contrast: "Dozens of messy spreadsheets for billboard bookings?" transitions cleanly into an ultra-sleek, dark-mode CRM dashboard with glowing live metrics and vacancy indicators.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Live Inventory Grid**: Interactive billboard cards showcasing location, format (Unipole/Gantry), dimensions, and real-time "Available / Booked" badges.
- **Instant Quotation Engine**: Fast calculation of campaign rates, taxes, and automatic quotation status updates.
- **Verified Proof of Display**: Geo-tagged mounting photos confirming live campaigns with a single click.

## Outro / punchline
"Out-of-Home advertising, unified. Engineered by Binjwa IT Solutions."

## User flow worth showing
Browse high-traffic billboard inventory → Generate dynamic quotation in 1 click → Track campaign execution and live proof of display.
